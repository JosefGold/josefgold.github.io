Levels and Expectations: A Software Engineer's Career Journey

  • Careers
  • Leadership

With David Ostrovsky

In the dynamic world of software engineering, understanding the trajectory of your career can be daunting. How does one progress from a junior engineer to a senior engineer? Is management the only path forward? And what can your manager do to help you grow as an engineer? These questions often puzzle both software developers and engineering managers alike.

Join us in this talk as we bring together a seasoned software engineer and an R&D manager, with close to 40 years combined industry experience, to discuss the real life intricacies of career progression for software engineers and share insights from both the senior engineer and managerial perspectives.

We will explore the meaning behind job titles and the expectations that come with them, uncovering practical milestones for career advancement. Discover how to craft your own roadmap for personal growth, foster crucial skills of both the “hard” and “soft” kind, and how to enlist your managers and peers to help you level up.

Whether you’re a software developer seeking clarity or a manager looking to support your team’s growth, this talk will equip you with the knowledge and language to navigate the levels and expectations of software engineering.
