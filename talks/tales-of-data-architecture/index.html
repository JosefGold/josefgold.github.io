Tales of Data Architecture Evolution

  • Data architecture
  • Data platforms

Data and Data Engineering in particular are fast becoming some of the most complex, interesting and important parts of every large scale system architecture. From the humble RDBMS server to massive Data Lakes and Data Meshes, everyone is somewhere on their path to the “Shangri-La” of data.

In this talk, we will try to look past the myriad of buzzwords and technologies to understand the fundamental reasons for the emergence of concepts such as the Data Warehouse, Data Lake and Data Mesh. Understand how to correctly approach architecting the analytical data infrastructure of a modern system, how to correctly evolve existing ones, and a glimpse to how the future of data platforms is going to look.
