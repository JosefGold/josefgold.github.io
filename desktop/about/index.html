About Josef (Yossi) Goldstein

The data and culture guy - and why that isn't a contradiction.

I got into engineering because I love how things work. I stayed because I love how people work. Turns out those two worlds have more in common than you'd think.

Most people call me in for the data infrastructure. They seem surprised when culture comes with it. I've stopped being surprised that they're surprised.

Fifteen-plus years of building the thing and helping fix the org around it. Big-data pipelines, real-time systems, teams that survive founders leaving. The parts that scale and the parts that don't. Both are the job.

These days I lead the Big Data Platform group at Wix. Before that, a run of international unicorn startups - mostly backend, mostly data, always the people around it.

Three lanes

Hired on the technical card. Kept for the other two.

  • Technical acumen. Infrastructure and big data depth. Can go hands-on and deep.
  • Vision & strategy. Sees where the domain is going long-term, and says it in a way that makes others see it too.
  • Culture & people. Builds strong teams. Culture is the third leg, not an afterthought.

What I actually do

I'm all about cutting the bullshit and talking about things plainly. Get good smart people to believe in you, tell them the truth, solve real problems together, teach them what they didn't learn yet.
